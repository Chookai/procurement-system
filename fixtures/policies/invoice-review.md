# Synthetic team policy: invoice review

This is a fictional learning fixture, not ABL policy.

## Invoice evidence
Eva reviews supplier invoices against the issued purchase order and accepted goods receipts or service milestones.
An invoice may arrive before the goods receipt. Missing acceptance evidence needs clarification.
Invoice review does not authorize payment, perform reconciliation or close outstanding delivery quantities.

## Partial delivery example
If ten pump seals are ordered and six are accepted, four remain outstanding.
A MYR 1,200 invoice for six accepted seals does not fulfil the other four seals.
