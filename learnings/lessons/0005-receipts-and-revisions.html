Receive against issued terms.

Keep delivery facts separate from a proposed change to the order.

Your win: calculate what Dinesh may accept while Ben reviews an increased quantity.

Generally: acceptance fulfils the order

Issued revision 1

10 ordered · 6 accepted

2 damaged · 1 rejected

Outstanding: 10 − 6 = 4

Proposed revision 2

12 ordered · awaiting Ben

Extra quantity is proposed, not issued.

Dinesh still has 4 outstanding.

Damaged and rejected quantities need replacement or resolution. They do not count as accepted. A service acceptance uses whole completed milestones rather than physical damaged-goods quantities. These are our recorded business rules, documented in the receiving walkthrough.

Follow the two workstreams

Ben reviews an increase to 12

Issued revision 1 still orders 10. After 6 accepted, Dinesh can record up to 4 more against it. Proposed quantities grant no receiving authority.

Chloe issues the approved increase

Revision 2 now orders 12. The same 6 accepted leaves 6 outstanding. The original receipt stays linked to revision 1; a new receipt uses revision 2.

A decrease becomes invalid before issuance

Ben approves a decrease to 8. Before Chloe issues it, Dinesh accepts 9 against the still-active order of 10. Issuance must fail: the replacement cannot order fewer than the 9 already accepted. Ben returns the proposal; Chloe submits corrected terms.

Trace the components

Receiving.tsx: ReceiptForm collects inspection quantities
  → main.tsx receive() → POST /requisitions/{id}/receipts
contracts.py: validate fields, unique lines and notes
workflow.py: verify receiver, lock PR and check version
receiving.py:
  check issued revision and outstanding quantities
  insert receipt with the original issued snapshot
workflow.py:
  update PR version + audit + retry response
  commit together

When Chloe issues a revision:
  validate_replacement() rechecks actual accepted quantities

Receipt posting and revision issuance share the PR row lock and command version. This serializes their writes; the losing stale command refreshes before retrying. The transaction commits receipt, version, audit and saved retry result together, or rolls them all back. PostgreSQL 17: Transactions.

Azure-specific: preserve the same business boundary

In the proposed Azure architecture, Azure Database for PostgreSQL would host these receipt and revision tables. The same FastAPI authorization and database transaction would enforce the rules. Azure hosting does not decide which quantity Dinesh may accept; the application’s issued PO and receipt records do.

We are running locally. Managed Azure hosting, identity integration and Foundry remain future work; this lesson does not deploy them.
