An issued PO is a snapshot →

Trace the new React form, API contract and transaction. Keep the approved estimate distinct from issued commercial terms.

04 · About 5 minutes

Approval is not issuance →

Trace a proposed PO revision through review and issuance, and identify which terms remain authoritative at each step.

05 · About 5 minutes

Receive against issued terms →

Separate accepted quantities, outstanding fulfilment and a pending PO change. Learn why issuance must check again after approval.

06 · About 5 minutes

Review is not payment →

Trace an invoice review without closing delivery quantities, and distinguish a retry from a duplicate supplier invoice.
